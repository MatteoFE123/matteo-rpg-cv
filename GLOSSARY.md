# Matteo RPG CV

Professional facts expressed through an optional tabletop-fantasy narrative.
Fantasy labels refer to the same facts, not to fictional achievements.

## Language

**Profile**:
Matteo's professional identity and summary of his AI development practice.
_Avoid_: Player account, fictional character biography

**Trajectory**:
The sequence of Matteo's employment roles, dates and responsibilities.
_Avoid_: Project list, campaign score

**Project**:
A concrete case of work described in the CV, which may belong to a role in the **Trajectory**.
_Avoid_: Employer, job, unverified public demo

**Skill**:
A named technology or professional capability evidenced by the CV.
_Avoid_: Ability score, proficiency percentage, character level

**Public CV**:
A downloadable professional account of the **Profile**, **Trajectory**, **Skills** and education,
prepared without the original document's private contact details.
_Avoid_: Original PDF, character sheet export

**RPG label**:
A narrative name paired with a plain professional term, such as a chronicle for **Trajectory**.
_Avoid_: Achievement claim, game rule
