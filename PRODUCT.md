# Matteo RPG CV

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Confirmed: Astro, TypeScript and native CSS, with small client-side interactions.
Static deployment to GitHub Pages through GitHub Actions. No backend, accounts or visitor tracking.
Astro 7 uses a project-local Node 22.23.4 runtime so the shared system runtime is not changed.
The older compatible Astro release was rejected because it carries published dependency advisories.

## Users

Recruiters and technical hiring managers evaluating Matteo Fecci for AI development roles.
They should understand his profile, find concrete work and reach his contact in less than a minute.

## Product Purpose

Present a real professional CV through an original tabletop-fantasy experience.
The RPG layer invites exploration without requiring a game, unlocking content or interpreting invented scores.

## Positioning

Matteo's actual multi-agent systems, backend work and production infrastructure are the substance.
Fantasy is a narrative lens, not a substitute for evidence.

## Operating Context

Visitors may scan on a phone, read on desktop or download a professional CV.
Spanish and English offer equivalent content. LinkedIn is the public contact channel.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Profile, employment trajectory, skills, projects, education and certifications remain clearly named.
- Skill filters and project disclosures are optional; keyboard users can reach all content.
- Motion is lightweight and respects reduced-motion preferences. No actual game, autoplay audio or 3D engine.
- Downloads are newly generated public PDFs, excluding telephone, personal email and contact locality.
- The original privately supplied PDF must never enter the repository, build output or Git history.
- No invented proficiency levels, outcomes, metrics, public project links or language qualifications.
- The user confirms that the UNIR master's degree is completed.

## Brand Commitments

Original fantasy inspired by tabletop RPGs in the spirit of DnD; no copied game art or official branding.
Matteo Fecci and AI Developer are always visible as the professional identity.
Reading this as: a developer portfolio for hiring teams, with tabletop-fantasy language and five separate catalog-derived systems.
Design variance 6, motion intensity 5, visual density 4: the user requested more dynamic, world-specific animation.
Bounded entrances and interactive transitions must preserve scannable facts and reduced-motion support.

## Evidence on Hand

The privately supplied two-page CV provides roles, dates, responsibilities, skills and education.
Its claims are self-reported, not independently verified. Master's completion was confirmed by the user.
Public contact: https://www.linkedin.com/in/matteo-fecci/
No portrait, public project screenshots or verified numerical outcomes were supplied.

## Product Principles

1. Professional clarity before fantasy vocabulary.
2. Exploration is optional; evidence is not locked.
3. Publish only approved, public-safe material.
4. Keep factual content equivalent across languages.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Responsive layouts, semantic landmarks, visible focus, keyboard-operable controls,
readable contrast and reduced-motion support.
